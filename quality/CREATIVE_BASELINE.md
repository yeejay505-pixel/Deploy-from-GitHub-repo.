# Existing engine: creative review baseline

The owner approved the visual design and animation of the v05 commercial
off-plan supply-gap video on 3 October 2026. It supersedes the earlier Yards
and commercial-office samples as the visual reference. This baseline belongs
to Animated AI Explainer Videos and does not certify automatic quality on new
topics. Final voice, evidence verification and publication remain separate
checks. See `approved-visual-reference.json` for the exact reference hash and
`UNIVERSAL_INTAKE_SETUP.md` for the next integration step.

## Design and motion

- White background; sage illustration surfaces; dark green artwork and charcoal
  text. Use the existing `LIGHT_PALETTE` instead of adding colors per scene.
- One clear headline, one main illustration, restrained captions and adequate
  space. Fit titles into at most two lines and avoid crowded paragraphs.
- Draw specific concepts with the existing vector objects and icon vocabulary.
  Preserve object identity as state changes. Explain cause before consequence.
- Drive counters and quantity drawings from the same metric. Avoid unrelated
  decorative movement or numerical jumps without an explained transition.
- Place click, arrival, whoosh and resolve sounds at the action they describe.
- Draft narration and visuals may be reviewed with SFX only. Retiming to actual
  voice and word timestamps is required before final release.

## Context imagery

Use supplied project imagery for claims about a particular development. Label
models and artist impressions accurately. Relevant licensed stock may support
general scenes such as business activity, commuting or office interiors; it must
not imply that an unrelated building or office is the actual project.

Keep text on clean white/sage surfaces. Use a framed image region or a readable
overlay rather than placing small text directly on busy footage. Slow crop/zoom
is available through offline local plates. Media provenance and license records
must accompany stock assets before publication; this does not add automatic
stock retrieval to the live planner.

## Source and review

Extract a claim ledger before animation planning. Preserve page references.
Flag conflicting facts in the source and leave unresolved amounts, dates and
project details out of the preview. Financial projections must remain identified
as projections and cannot become assured outcomes in the script.

The quality review checks visual relevance, narrative order, readability,
animation continuity and SFX timing. User review of a sample is separate from
live workflow validation. The next architecture integration is Workflow 00's
durable intake gateway, followed by a renderer binding that can execute the
approved richer visual profile. The older limited semantic preview remains a
separate draft and cannot serve as an automatic quality fallback. Final voice,
source verification and publication remain separate stages.
